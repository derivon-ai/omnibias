trademarks of **Derivon** ("we", "us"). This policy explains how
you may and may not use the Marks. It is independent of the software license:
the [AGPL](LICENSE) and the [commercial license](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md) grant
rights to the **code**, not to the **Marks**.

This policy is modeled on common open-source trademark policies (e.g. those
of the PSF and Linux Foundation projects) and is **not legal advice**.

## You may, without asking

- State **truthfully** that your product "uses omnibias", "is built on
  omnibias", or "is compatible with omnibias" (nominative use).
- Use the name to refer to the project in articles, talks, tutorials, and
  academic citations.
- Redistribute **unmodified** official packages under their existing name.

## You may not, without written permission

- Use the Marks (or confusingly similar names) as the **name of your own
  product, company, or service**, or in a way that implies endorsement,
  sponsorship, or affiliation.
- Use the Marks on **modified** distributions in a way that suggests they are
  the official build. If you ship a modified version, please rename it.
- Register the Marks (or confusingly similar marks, domains, or social
  handles) anywhere in the world.
- Use the logo in a modified form, or in a manner that disparages the
  project.

## Modified versions

If you distribute a modified version (as the AGPL permits), you must not
present it as the official omnibias. Give your fork a distinct name and make
clear it is unofficial and derived from omnibias.
