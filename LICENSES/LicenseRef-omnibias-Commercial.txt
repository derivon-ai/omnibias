This is a *license reference*, not a license grant. It exists so that the SPDX
expression

is resolvable by REUSE-compliant and SPDX-aware tooling. It documents the

Which files this applies to
---------------------------

The copyleft tier of omnibias -- the packages recorded as `copyleft` in
`[tool.omnibias.license_tiers]` in the repository root `pyproject.toml`. Every
file in those packages carries the SPDX header

    SPDX-License-Identifier: AGPL-3.0-or-later OR LicenseRef-omnibias-Commercial

The permissive tier (recorded as `permissive` in the same table) is
Apache-2.0 only and is **never** covered by this reference. You never need a
commercial license to use a permissive-tier package.


What the expression means
-------------------------

You may use the copyleft tier under **either** branch, at your option:

1. **AGPL-3.0-or-later** -- the terms in `LICENSES/AGPL-3.0-or-later.txt`.
   Free of charge. You accept the copyleft obligations, including the AGPL
   Section 13 network-use source-disclosure requirement.

2. **LicenseRef-omnibias-Commercial** -- a separate, signed, written commercial
   license agreement issued by Derivon. Without such an executed agreement,
   this branch grants nothing and the AGPL branch is the only one available to
   you.

Selecting branch 2 requires an executed agreement. Merely reading this file,
downloading the software, or paying an invoice does not execute one.
