# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

omnibias ships in two licence tiers — a permissive **Apache-2.0** tier and a
dual-licensed **AGPL-3.0-or-later or commercial** tier (see
[`LICENSING.md`](../LICENSING.md)). To keep both possible from one codebase,
every contributor must agree to this Contributor License Agreement before their
contribution can be merged. The CLA is **repository-wide**: it covers a
contribution to either tier, because a contribution may be moved between tiers
as packages evolve.

This CLA is adapted from the widely used Apache Software Foundation
Individual/Corporate CLAs. It lets the project license your contribution under
Apache-2.0, the AGPL, and commercial terms. **You keep the copyright to your
contribution** — you are granting a license, not assigning ownership.

> This document is not legal advice. If you are contributing on behalf of an
> employer, make sure you have authority to do so (see "Corporate CLA").

## How to sign

Contributions are gated by an automated CLA check on every pull request. On
your first PR, the bot will ask you to accept this CLA by commenting:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your acceptance (GitHub username, name, and timestamp) is recorded in the
project's signed-CLA registry. By signing, you agree to the terms below.
